THE PROBLEM & THE PHILOSOPHY

Expense tracking shouldn't mean sacrificing your privacy.

Most financial apps require connecting live bank credentials, read your entire SMS inbox, and upload your transaction history to cloud servers for advertising. Spendo changes the paradigm.

CONVENTIONAL TRACKERS
  • ✕Invasive Cloud Uploads: Your entire purchase ledger and net worth are stored on third-party servers.
  • ✕Overreaching SMS Permissions: Apps scan your entire personal messaging history under the guise of banking SMS detection.
  • ✕Manual Logging Burnout: Typing every coffee and grocery bill leads to tracker abandonment within weeks.
  • ✕Monetized with Ads & Loans: Clean interfaces are gradually replaced with credit card advertisements and loan promotions.
THE SPENDO ARCHITECTURE
  • ✓100% On-Device Storage: All accounts, budgets, and transactions live strictly inside your local Android Room SQLite database.
  • ✓Selective Notification Ingestion: Spendo uses Android's NotificationListenerService only for apps you explicitly select. No SMS permission required.
  • ✓One-Tap Voice Logging: Speak outlays in natural language ("₹450 for lunch at Swiggy"); Spendo drafts the entry for confirmation.
  • ✓Deterministic Answering: Spendo AI queries your verified database deterministically. Never hallucinates numbers.

VERIFIED PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

Your data never leaves your device.

We believe personal finance software should be a local vault, not a cloud surveillance engine. Here is our exact technical commitment.

100% On-Device Room Database

Every account, category, budget, and transaction record is stored directly on your phone in an encrypted local Room SQLite database. We do not operate a backend server, user database, or central ledger.

Zero Inbound SMS Permissions

Unlike traditional Indian expense apps, Spendo never requests READ_SMS permission. It utilizes Android's NotificationListenerService exclusively for banking apps you manually select in settings.

No Account, Login, or KYC Needed

You do not need to register with your phone number, email, or Google account. Spendo works the moment you install it. Your financial life belongs to you.

Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) AI

Local financial questions are answered deterministically offline. For cloud LLM exploration (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI), your API keys are stored securely using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences.

Android Cloud Backup Disabled

Spendo explicitly disables standard Android cloud backup (android:allowBackup=false) to ensure unencrypted transaction dumps are never uploaded to third-party cloud drives without your knowledge.

Full Data Custody & Export

Export all your transactions anytime to clean, standard UTF-8 CSV files via Android's Storage Access Framework. You can purge or backup your ledger in a single tap.
